Settings & saves

Progress saves automatically on this device (money, upgrades, filmography, reviews, recorded takes). Whoever joins also keeps a copy of the studio, so either founder can host next time.

How to play
  • Writers' Room: start an original, a sequel to one of your own films or a famous blockbuster, or buy a pitch from the writers who wander into the lobby. Their estimates are risky!
  • Sound Stage: pick a scene, camera angle, set and lighting, then shout ACTION. Your movements, voices and VFX are recorded as a take.
  • Edit Bay: drop takes on the timeline and cut it down to 60 seconds using only Split, Cut, Duplicate and Chroma Key. Add copyright-free music, then export the film to your device.
  • Release: one of you goes on The Daisy Bloom Show. Answer fast: dead air kills your confidence and your box office. Then read your reviews on ReelBuzz.
  • Story: Gill, a 400-year-old goldfish producer, phones you with chapters to beat while the rival Megaplex Pictures tries to crush you. Random events land in your phone inbox.
  • Desktop: click to look, WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, click screens with the crosshair, 1–5 acting gestures, F fire VFX, R record/cut on set, P phone, G drop held prop, hold M to mute, Esc free mouse, H help.
  • VR: left stick moves, right stick snap-turns, trigger clicks screens, A fires VFX, B Action/Cut, X shows/hides your phone (map, social, inbox).

Controls

  • Click the world to capture the mouse, Esc to release it.
  • WASD / arrows move · Shift run · Space jump.
  • Aim the crosshair at any screen and click to press its buttons.
  • Acting: 1 wave · 2 point · 3 punch · 4 hero pose · 5 cheer.
  • F fires the selected VFX · R Action/Cut on the stage · P phone · G drop held prop · T next room.
  • Muting is deliberately hard: hold M for one second (or confirm the HUD button). Tap M to unmute.
  • VR: left stick moves, right stick turns, trigger clicks, A = VFX, B = Action/Cut, X = phone.
  • Your voice is proximity chat: walk up to your co-founder to talk.
